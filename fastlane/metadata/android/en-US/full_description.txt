Tatrak is an Android messenger for the open XMPP network that keeps your data encrypted not only in transit, but also on the phone itself.

Encrypted on the device:
* The whole message history is stored in an encrypted database (SQLCipher)
* Attachments, voice messages, camera photos, avatars, wallpapers and sticker packs are stored encrypted too
* The key is random and protected by the Android Keystore
* There is no app passcode yet: the encryption protects the stored data, not an unlocked phone in someone else's hands

End-to-end encryption with OMEMO and OpenPGP comes from Conversations unchanged.

Also a familiar, Telegram-like interface:
* Chat folders: All, Personal, Groups and Unread; archive, pin, mute and select several chats at once
* Search across conversations, replies, reactions, editing and delete for everyone
* Sticker packs, voice messages and attachment previews in the chat
* Encrypted audio and video calls
* Light and dark themes, accent colours and chat wallpapers
* Several accounts, group chats and push notifications through UnifiedPush, no Google account needed

Tatrak is built on Conversations and works with any standard XMPP server: pick a provider or run your own. Available features depend on the server.

Tatrak is free software under the GNU GPL v3.
