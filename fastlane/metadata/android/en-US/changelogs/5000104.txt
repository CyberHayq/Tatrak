- First F-Droid release, based on Conversations 2.21.0 with its OMEMO and OpenPGP unchanged
- Data on the device is encrypted: history in SQLCipher; attachments, voice messages, avatars and stickers in encrypted files
- New interface: chat folders, archive, multi-select, replies, swipe to reply, delete for everyone, sticker packs, nine accents, AMOLED night theme
- The message menu shows send and receive times and the encrypting key
- Restores Conversations and monocles backups
